01 / PREDICTION WORKSPACE

Drug Synergy Prediction

Enter a drug name or molecular SMILES for each compound, then choose a cell line. The final score comes from an XGBoost model that reads the Drug Resistance Signature of both compounds, the method used in the research paper.

RECORDED COMBINATION EXAMPLES

Try a combination

Recorded combinations pooled from Almanac, DrugComb, DrugCombDB, OncologyScreen and Oneil. Reversed pairs and cross-dataset repeats are collapsed into a single entry. Selecting one fills both drugs and the cell line, ready to submit to the DRS model.

02 / RECORDED EVALUATION

Structure, DS, and DRS

One grouped 80/20 holdout per dataset, split by canonical unordered drug pair so no pair appears in both training and test. The DRS model is the one that serves predictions; Structure and DS are baselines trained in the same run.
